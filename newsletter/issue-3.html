Lead story: the band stays together

The Nationals plan to bring back all 12 coaches from manager Blake Butera’s 2026 staff, MLB Trade Rumors reported Oct. 22 (via Spencer Nusbaum of The Athletic). That includes 30-year-old pitching coach Simon Mathews and hitting coaches Matt Borgschulte, Andrew Ayt and Sean O’Malley.

The changes are on the training side. The club declined to renew head strength coach Brett Henry, head athletic trainer Dale Gilbert, and assistant trainers Jonathan Kotredes and Eric Montague. Director of medical services Harvey Sharman left in September. Per the report, the split was amicable, and the team plans to add biomechanics resources this winter (Yahoo Sports).

  • 77-85 in 2026: an 11-win improvement and the most wins since 2019 (MLB.com).
  • Finished strong: 10 wins in the last 14 games, after an 11-18 August (MLBTR).

“We’ve got to figure out how to get better because we just felt like we left a lot of meat on the bone.”Blake Butera, via MLB.com (Jessica Camerato)

Our take: Keeping the coaches is a vote of confidence in a staff that got an 11-win jump out of the youngest group of position players in the league. Now the front office has to give them more pitching to work with.

Five questions for the winter

From MLB.com’s offseason preview:

  • Free agency: Paul Toboni likes the payroll flexibility and says there are “creative ways” to use it. That could mean signings, extensions or both. The Banner notes a possible lockout hangs over everything (the labor deal expires Dec. 1).
  • CJ Abrams: An All-Star starter coming off a career year, and expected to be in trade buzz again. He played 14 games at second base with a .987 fielding percentage. No. 3 prospect Seaver King is projected to debut in 2027.
  • Veteran leadership: Butera said a veteran voice would have helped during three straight blown saves against the Phillies in June and the deadline skid.
  • Jacob Young: The Yankees have checked in on the center fielder, per Joel Sherman of the New York Post (via MLBTR). It’s unclear whether that was real talks or just due diligence.

Our take: Abrams at second and King at short would be a fun 2027 middle infield. We’d rather watch that than read trade rumors all winter.

The NL East group chat Light ribbing, real numbers

The World Series opens tonight, Oct. 23. Number of NL East teams in it: zero. The Phillies scored 9 runs in three Wild Card games and went home (MLB.com). The Mets didn’t make the postseason and are now at 40 years since their last title in 1986.

Our take: The Nats have a title more recent than either of them. 2019, baby. We bring it up only out of love. 😄
