Issue #1Week of Oct 15, 2026

Fall League heat, a farm that won, and an offseason to plan

Welcome

Welcome to the first issue of our family Nats newsletter! Each week you'll get a quick rundown of the Nationals farm system: who's hot, who moved, and what the front office is weighing. The big leaguers are home for the winter, but the pipeline isn't. Nats prospects are in the Arizona Fall League, Rochester just won a title, and the Top 30 has plenty to talk about.

Ranks are MLB Pipeline's Nationals Top 30. Stat lines are 2026 regular-season totals across all levels, from the MLB Stats API.

Prospects to watch

#1Eli Willits, SS

AA/A+/A · .265/.406/.463 · 14 HR · 62 SB

The top prospect in the system climbed three levels in 2026 and was assigned to Double-A Harrisburg on Sept. 8. A .406 on-base percentage and 62 steals is a table-setter's stat line.

#4Jarlin Susana, RHP

AAA/AA/A+ · 6.20 ERA · 20.1 IP · 37 K · 1.43 WHIP

The regular season was short at just 20.1 innings, but the strikeouts (37) jump off the page, and the fall has been all Susana. MLB.com named him Arizona Fall League Pitcher of the Week, and his high-octane velocity headlined Week 1's top performers. MLB.com also covered him hitting 104 on the radar gun at the Triple-A National Championship.

#3Seaver King, SS/2B

AAA/AA · .303/.382/.516 · 21 HR · 15 SB

King hit .300 with 20-homer power between Double-A and Triple-A. He finished the season at Rochester, one step from Washington.

#10Miguel Sime Jr., RHP

A+/A · 2.99 ERA · 72.1 IP · 130 K · 1.41 WHIP

Sime piled up 130 strikeouts in 72.1 innings, about 16 per nine. The walks and hits (1.41 WHIP) are what to watch, but that kind of swing-and-miss gets noticed.

#9Jackson Kent, LHP

MLB/AAA/AA · 4.34 ERA · 130.2 IP · 135 K · 1.31 WHIP

Kent pitched at Double-A, Triple-A and in the majors. Washington recalled him from Rochester on Sept. 28 for the final weekend.

Key moves

It was a quiet week on the transaction wire. These are the most recent moves involving Top 30 prospects and the farm:

  • Oct 7: Rochester RHPs Max Kranick and Justin Lawrence elected free agency.
  • Oct 5: Rochester activated OF #28 Sam Petersen from the 7-day injured list. He hit .248/.374/.417 with 16 HR and 38 SB at AAA/AA.
  • Sept 28: Washington recalled LHP #9 Jackson Kent and RHP #12 Luis Perales from Rochester.
  • Sept 26: RHP #14 Jack Cebert was assigned to Rochester from Harrisburg. He has 122 K in 109.1 IP across three levels.
  • Sept 15: RHP #4 Jarlin Susana was assigned to Rochester from Harrisburg.

Source: MLB Stats API transactions.
