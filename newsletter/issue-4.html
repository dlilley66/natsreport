Lead story: first move, full house

The Nationals made their first move of the offseason on Oct. 29, claiming right-hander Braxton Roxby off waivers from the Giants and designating Zak Kent for assignment to open a 40-man spot (MLB Trade Rumors).

  • Roxby: made his big league debut in late August and threw 5 1/3 innings for San Francisco (2 runs, 3 strikeouts). He had a 3.78 ERA at Triple-A Sacramento, leans on his slider, and still has three minor league options left.
  • Kent: a 5.51 ERA in 16 1/3 big league innings this year between Washington and Minnesota, and he’s out of options next year (MLBTR).
  • Nobody left: when free agency opened, the Nats had zero free agents from their 40-man roster (TalkNats). That means a crowded roster and lots of decisions for Paul Toboni before a possible lockout (Federal Baseball).

Meanwhile, the trade chatter around CJ Abrams keeps going. Yahoo Sports asked what it would take for the Cardinals to trade for him. He’s coming off 33 homers and 106 RBI.

Our take: A waiver claim with three options left is a smart, low-risk way to add bullpen depth. The bullpen was the weak spot in 2026, so expect more moves like this, plus, we hope, one big arm for the back end.

The NL East group chat Light ribbing, real numbers

The Milwaukee Brewers won the World Series 4 games to 1 over the Rays, their first title ever, after going 11-2 in the postseason (MLB.com, NBC Sports). On their way, they struck out the Phillies plenty: Jacob Misiorowski fanned 15 Phillies in one June start (see the memes below).

Final 2026 NL East standings: Nats 77-85, Mets 74-88. That’s right, the Mets finished last, three games behind us. The Phillies won 88 and scored 9 runs in three Wild Card games before heading home.

Our take: Third place isn’t a parade, but it’s ahead of Queens. We bring it up only out of love. 😄
